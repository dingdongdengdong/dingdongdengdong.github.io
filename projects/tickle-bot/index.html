SB-P2-18 · Paper 00043“Tickle Bot: A Low-Cost Physical AI Research Platform”

Wook Ko · Dongje Lee · Yoonchang Choi · Heejae Jung · Seojeong Park

01 / Research question

How can more students experiment with Physical AI without starting from expensive hardware?

The project frames the robot itself as research infrastructure: a lower-cost platform where AI algorithms, ROS2 interfaces, and physical behavior can be studied together.

Embodied research is constrained by access to machines that can expose sensing, control, and software integration in a form students can modify. Tickle Bot focuses on that access problem rather than presenting a single benchmark task.

The public material supports the platform goal, paper authorship, venue, and award. It does not expose enough detail to claim individual subsystem ownership or quantitative performance.
