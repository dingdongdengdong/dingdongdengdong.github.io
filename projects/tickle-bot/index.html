Tickle Bot

A collaborative low-cost Physical AI research platform presented at KIIS 2025.

low-cost Physical AI research platform

Tickle Bot is a collaborative research and experimental platform designed to lower the cost barrier for students and researchers who want to test Physical AI and robotics algorithms on a real robotic system.

Outstanding Paper Award · KIIS Fall Conference 2025

why it matters

Physical AI research often requires expensive robot platforms. Tickle Bot explores a lower-cost platform supporting experimentation with robot control, AI integration, ROS2, and embodied intelligence.
