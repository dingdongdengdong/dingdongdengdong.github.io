## Low-cost Physical AI research platform

Tickle Bot is a collaborative research and experimental platform designed to lower the cost barrier for students and researchers who want to test Physical AI and robotics algorithms on a real robotic system.

The work was presented at the **2025 Fall Conference of the Korean Institute of Intelligent Systems (KIIS)** and received an **Outstanding Paper Award**.

### Why it matters

Physical AI research often requires expensive robot platforms. Tickle Bot explores a lower-cost platform that can support experimentation with robot control, AI integration, ROS2, and embodied intelligence.
