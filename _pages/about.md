I am a robotics engineer and builder working at the intersection of **robot learning, physical systems, and product execution**. My current technical focus is learning-based manipulation on custom robot hardware: teleoperation, imitation learning, simulation, and sim-to-real validation.

I study **Robot Software Engineering at Soongsil University** and also pursue **Next-Generation Semiconductor Engineering**. I am especially interested in Embodied AI, Vision-Language-Action models, manipulation, synthetic data, and efficient deployment on real robots.

Beyond research, I operate **[Revive Robotics](https://revive-robotics.onrender.com/)**, a live robotics-focused web product. I enjoy taking ideas all the way from hardware and software prototypes to systems that people can actually use.

### Current focus

- Custom 5-DoF robot arm + AmazingHand integration
- Meta Quest 2 VR teleoperation and robot demonstration collection
- LeRobot datasets, ACT, and VLA-based manipulation policies
- Isaac Sim / MuJoCo simulation and sim-to-real workflows
- Edge deployment with Jetson and robotics middleware

### Research interests

**Robot Learning · Embodied AI · Robot Manipulation · Sim-to-Real · Physical AI · Vision-Language-Action**

### Selected work

- **Custom Robot Learning Platform** — VR teleoperation → dataset → policy → physical deployment
- **Tickle Bot** — collaborative low-cost Physical AI research platform; KIIS 2025 Outstanding Paper Award
- **Aerial Sim-to-Real Inspection** — synthetic data, PX4/ROS2, perception, and Jetson deployment
- **Revive Robotics** — a robotics product I build and operate
