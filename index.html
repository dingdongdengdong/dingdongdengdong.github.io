04 / About

I care about the full robot stack.

My interests sit at the boundary between robot learning and systems engineering: how a policy is trained matters, but so do embodiment, control interfaces, data quality, simulation fidelity, and deployment constraints.

I am particularly interested in manipulation, embodied AI, VLA models, synthetic data, and sim-to-real evaluation on custom robotic platforms.
