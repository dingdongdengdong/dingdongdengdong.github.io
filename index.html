Dongje Lee

Robotics Engineer · Embodied AI Researcher · Entrepreneur

I build robotic systems at the intersection of robot learning, physical systems, and product execution. My current focus is learning-based manipulation on custom hardware: teleoperation, imitation learning, simulation, and sim-to-real validation.

I study Robot Software Engineering at Soongsil University and also pursue Next-Generation Semiconductor Engineering. I am especially interested in Embodied AI, Vision-Language-Action models, manipulation, synthetic data, and efficient deployment on real robots.

Beyond research, I operate Revive Robotics, a live robotics-focused product. I like taking ideas from technical prototypes to systems and services that people can actually use.
