04Full-road proof

Echo Full
Isaac Sim Self-Drive

A portable Isaac Sim 5.1 autonomous mobile-robot stack with ROS 2 Jazzy, Nav2, and RTX LiDAR.

The system drives a differential-drive proxy while a preserved Echo CAD shell follows measured PhysX pose. Its recorded validation run covers pedestrian stop/clear, LiDAR-based detour, two speed bumps, a right turn, uneven and unpaved road sections, and a terminal stop.

04 / About

I care about the full robot stack.

My interests sit at the boundary between robot learning and systems engineering: how a policy is trained matters, but so do embodiment, control interfaces, data quality, simulation fidelity, and deployment constraints.

My research interests are Robot Manipulation, Simulation-to-Real, and Learning-Based Control, with a focus on learning and evaluating manipulation policies for custom robotic platforms.
